Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting a project with Chirag Tech Dev.

How do I start a project with Chirag Tech Dev?
You can begin by selecting a service package or filling out our quick project inquiry form above. Once submitted, we will review your requirements on WhatsApp or Email and provide a customized quote and timeline.
How much does a website development cost?
Starter single-page business websites begin from ₹1,999+. Multi-page business websites with portfolio features start from ₹3,999+. The exact price depends on the number of pages, custom features, and design requirements.
How much does an Android app development cost?
Basic utility Android apps start from ₹2,999+. Features such as user login, cloud database integration, and complex dashboards are quoted individually based on exact specifications.
Can you maintain or upgrade my existing Android app?
Yes! Maintenance services start from ₹999+. We fix code bugs, update obsolete Android SDK libraries, modernize user interfaces, and introduce new features to your existing software.
Can you assist with publishing apps to Google Play Store?
Yes, Play Store publishing assistance starts from ₹999+. We guide you through preparing store graphics, app privacy policy generation, target audience declaration, and submission workflow. (Note: Final app approval rests solely with Google's policy team).
What are the payment terms for a project?
Projects typically follow an advance milestone structure (e.g., 50% initial advance to start development work, and 50% upon final project completion and testing prior to deployment).
